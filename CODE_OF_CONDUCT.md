ACWOL Code of Conduct
Conscience-Aligned Participation Protocol

ACWOL is not a conventional software repository.
It is a philosophical and AI ethics architecture centered on the disciplined mining of Intellect.
Participation within this space must therefore reflect intentional rigor, structural clarity, and conscience alignment.
This Code of Conduct is grounded in the Three-Way Impact Principle (3WIP).
I. The Three-Way Impact Principle (3WIP)
Before posting, proposing, or contributing, participants must internally validate their intention across three domains:

1️⃣ Self
Does this contribution refine understanding?
Does it elevate clarity rather than ego?
Is it intellectually disciplined rather than reactive?

2️⃣ Others
Does this contribution advance collective comprehension?
Does it engage respectfully and constructively?
Does it critique ideas without attacking individuals?

3️⃣ Long-Term System
Does this contribution strengthen the structural coherence of ACWOL?
Does it support responsible intelligence development?
Does it align with sustainable ethical architecture rather than short-term impulse?

If a contribution fails validation in any of these domains, it should be reconsidered before submission. Thank you.
